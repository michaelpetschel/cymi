Attribution: concept inspiration from the SSNC wizard (GPLv3) at damota.me/ssnc/wizard.

  1. Identify V1 or V2/Lite/OLED model.
  2. If V1, check serial range before selecting software paths.
  3. If V2 family, plan a hardware modchip route and required tooling.
  4. Validate firmware compatibility and follow trusted documentation.
